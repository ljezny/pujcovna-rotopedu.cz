Why rent a paddleboard (SUP)

  • Full-body workout: your muscles work almost constantly as you keep balancing on the water.
  • Balance and fitness: sharpen your sense of balance and strengthen your deep stabilising muscles.
  • Anyone can do it: you and your kids will pick up the technique in no time.
  • Easy to transport: packed in its backpack it weighs about 15 kg – you can even take it on the tram.
  • Everything included: pump, paddle and backpack.

Paddleboarding was invented by surf instructors in Hawaii, who paddled standing up on their boards so they could keep an eye on their students.

Today's paddleboard is an (inflatable) board that only loosely resembles a surfboard. In this fun sport, you move across the water using a long paddle.

Paddleboarding engages the muscles of your whole body almost constantly, because you have to keep balancing on the water. Riding the board improves your sense of balance, boosts your fitness and strengthens your deep stabilising muscles (the ones you only discover you have after a few hours of paddleboarding).

But it's not a difficult sport. You and your kids will master the technique easily. And worst case, you'll just fall in the water.
