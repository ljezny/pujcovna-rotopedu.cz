Want to really push yourself, boost your fitness and feel like you've had a proper workout? A spinning bike is the ideal choice if a classic exercise bike feels too easy and you're after higher resistance and intensity.

Your own pedalling drives a flywheel weighing over 15 kg, which ensures smooth motion and a realistic riding feel. The mechanical brake lets you set the resistance precisely – from a brisk ride to a seriously tough workout. Thanks to its sturdy steel frame, the bike stays solid even during intense out-of-the-saddle sprints.

Even so, the spinning bike is fully suited to home use. It's a bit louder than a classic exercise bike, but still fine for a flat. The bike is stable and doesn't take up unnecessary space. Thanks to its transport wheels, you can easily move it when cleaning.

Why rent a spinning bike

  • much higher resistance than an exercise bike
  • great for intense workouts and intervals
  • no big upfront purchase
  • a chance to find out whether this type of training suits you

Practical information

  • quiet operation, suitable for flats
  • stable frame, even for riding out of the saddle
  • easy to move thanks to its wheels
  • for transport, a regular car with the rear seats folded down is all you need

If you really want to put in the work at home and take your fitness further, a spinning bike is a better choice than an exercise bike.
