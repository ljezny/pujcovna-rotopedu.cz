Don't want to lose your form over the winter, but the idea of sitting on someone else's exercise bike doesn't appeal? A bike trainer (turbo trainer) is the ideal choice for riders who don't want to give up their own bike. No getting used to a different riding position, saddle or geometry. Just clamp your rear wheel into the trainer and ride exactly the way you do outdoors.

Why rent a bike trainer:

  • No compromise on ergonomics: You ride your own bike, the one you're used to. Your back and joints stay happy thanks to the fit you've already dialled in.
  • Use your own gears: On top of several resistance levels on the trainer itself, you can shift smoothly with your own derailleur – just like riding off-road or on the road.
  • Easy to store: When you're not training, simply fold the trainer and slide it under the bed or into a cupboard. It's the most space-saving option for home cycling.

The perfect partner for the winter months

The winter season lasts just a few months, and a quality trainer only takes up space for the rest of the year. Renting lets you ride through the winter on pro equipment and simply hand it back to us in spring. No summer storage, no maintenance to worry about.

Practical information and transport

To make your living-room training hassle-free, here are our recommendations:

  • Easy transport: The trainer is light and compact. It fits in any car, and if needed you can comfortably take it on public transport.
  • Slick tyre: We recommend fitting a slick (road) tyre on your rear wheel. It significantly reduces noise and vibration.
  • Floor protection: You can also rent a thin protective mat with the trainer. It keeps your floor safe from tyre dust or a drop of chain oil.

Tip for a better ride: Set the trainer up in front of your TV or tablet and play footage of a real route or a cycling race. Since you control your own gears, the ride will feel almost indistinguishable from the real thing.
