Want to get in shape, but bad weather, a crowded gym or simply the commute keep putting you off? Walking and running are the most natural movements for the human body. With a treadmill, you can enjoy them in total comfort. Set up a gym right in your living room and train whenever you feel like it and have the time – even while watching your favourite show.

We pick proven treadmill models for you (e.g. Kettler, Brother or Lifefit) that meet our strict standard for home training. No need to study spec sheets – we've done that for you.

Why rent a treadmill:

  • Plenty of room to run: The running surface is always over 40 cm wide – enough space that you don't have to watch every step.
  • High weight capacity: Our treadmills support at least 120 kg, often more. They suit taller and heavier users too.
  • Hills at the push of a button: Most treadmills let you change the incline with a single button while you run (for when you want to tackle a hill).
  • A smart coach in the console: Built-in programs can adjust both speed and incline. They add variety to your workout – but of course you can also just run at a steady pace.

What makes renting the smartest choice?

  • Test your willpower risk-free: Plenty of treadmills end up as clothes racks after a few weeks. Rent one on trial. If you get hooked, you can buy your own later. If not, simply return it – and you've saved thousands.
  • Perfect for winter or rehab: Need to get moving again after an injury, or just get through the winter months? Renting for a few weeks makes more sense than buying a machine that will be in your way the rest of the year.

Logistics and practical information

We know space and transport matter to you. That's why we choose models that are “just right”:

  • They fit through doors: Our treadmills fold up and, when folded, fit through standard 80 cm doors (often narrower ones too).
  • You can handle it: These aren't 150 kg commercial monsters. They also have wheels, so they're easy to move – when cleaning, for example. Often a single person can manage transport and handling.
  • Transport: An estate car is usually all you need. Our experienced team will help you load it – and trust us, we've loaded treadmills into all kinds of vehicles. Not sure? Give us a call and we'll advise you.

Rental tip: Live in a flat? We recommend adding a shock-absorbing mat. It protects your floor and significantly reduces vibrations – your downstairs neighbours will thank you.
