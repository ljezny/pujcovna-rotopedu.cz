Want to get the most out of exercising at home? An electronic exercise bike is the ideal choice if you're looking for high comfort and modern features. It gives you plenty of ways to tailor your workout – whether you just want to pedal casually in front of the TV or follow a smart, pre-set program.

Why rent an electronic exercise bike?

  • Resistance at your fingertips: Change the resistance easily with the buttons on the display. If you don't want to use the programs, you can raise or lower it manually at any time, depending on how you feel.
  • Motivating programs: Tired of keeping track of time and resistance yourself? Choose from several workout modes. The bike then adjusts the resistance for you and simulates, for example, riding through hilly terrain.
  • Efficient fat burning: Some models can adjust the resistance based on your current heart rate. Ideal if you want to lose weight or build fitness as efficiently as possible.
  • Clear overview: The large display is easy to read in any lighting and gives you accurate feedback on your performance.

Train at home, comfortable and dry

Buying your own electronic trainer is quite an investment. Renting lets you use a top-equipped machine only during the months you actually need it. After a few weeks, simply return it – no worries about maintenance or finding somewhere to store it.
