How did we come up with this?

I got the idea of renting out exercise bikes, fitness machines and rehabilitation aids after my own knee injury and the operations that followed. I left my first rehabilitation session determined to quickly get all the recommended equipment. The enthusiasm faded once I found out how much it all costs — especially since some of it is only needed for a few weeks of rehab.

Renting is a way to solve this. Customers get quality equipment and only pay for the time they really need it. They don't have to squeeze in among sweaty people at the gym, and above all their home doesn't fill up with unused clutter (who doesn't know the exercise bike that ends up as a permanent clothes rack). All of this for less than an average gym membership.

Of course, our exercise bikes and trainers aren't just for patients after surgery. Our rental is for anyone who wants to work out on a quality machine in the comfort of their home — whether you want to test your willpower before buying a machine for thousands, prefer to sweat in private, or simply don't feel like fighting over equipment at the gym.

Ondřej Bena, founder

Frequently asked questions

How much do I pay at hand-over?

You only pay a deposit at hand-over. The rental fee is deducted from the deposit and we return the rest to you.

Will you carry the machine upstairs?

We deliver machines to the first lockable door. On request we'll gladly carry them upstairs at no extra charge. The machine arrives assembled and adjusted.

Can I take the machine home in my car?

An exercise bike fits into a normal car with the rear seats folded down; for a treadmill an estate car is usually enough. We'll help you load it. If you're not sure, give us a call.

When can I pick up the machine?

We have no fixed opening hours and deliver machines during the day, so please call ahead to arrange a time for your visit.

Is an exercise bike suitable for rehabilitation?

Yes. A magnetic exercise bike is stable, gentle on the joints and quiet. An electronic exercise bike lets you control the resistance precisely, which makes it ideal for guided rehabilitation.

Can I extend the rental?

Yes, just let us know and we'll arrange the extension.

Do you offer gift vouchers?

Yes, two kinds. You can prepay the rental of a specific machine or paddleboard according to the price list (the voucher shows no amount, as a gift should), or buy a credit so the recipient can choose freely. We'll email the voucher to you for free, or you can collect it from us. Order by phone or using the form.

Where can I find out more about paddleboards?

For more information about paddleboards, see our other website www.paddleboardbrno.cz.

Do you deliver outside the listed cities?

For delivery prices outside these areas, contact us at +420 774 860 054.
