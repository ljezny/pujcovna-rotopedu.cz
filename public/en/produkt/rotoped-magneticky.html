Show more information

Looking for a reliable way to get your joints moving again after an injury, improve your fitness or burn calories at home? The magnetic exercise bike is a proven classic for home training. It's very quiet, easy to use, and since it doesn't need to be plugged in, you can put it anywhere in your home.

We have various types and sizes available, so we'll always pick a machine whose size and settings suit you.

A great model to start with

The magnetic exercise bike is the ideal choice if you're new to exercise or want to find out whether working out at home will keep you motivated in the long run. Renting means you don't have to start with an expensive investment.

If you later find you need more resistance or more motivation, we'll gladly swap it for an electronic exercise bike or another type of trainer.

Practical transport information

  • Easy transport: a regular car with the rear seats folded down is all you need.
  • Easy to move: transport wheels make it easy to push the bike aside.
  • Light and compact: it doesn't take up unnecessary space.

Rental tip

If you exercise on laminate, vinyl or another delicate floor covering, we recommend renting a protective mat with the bike. It helps protect your floor from scratches and makes cleaning around the machine easier. On tiles or carpet, a mat usually isn't needed.
