Want to burn calories and tone your muscles at the same time? Rowing is unique in that it works both your upper and lower body. It's the perfect mix of cardio and strength training. It may not look like it, but with the right technique you heavily engage your core and legs, supported by your arms and back. Simply put, it's one of the most complete machines you can get for your home.

It's ideal both for beginners who want to shed pounds fast and for serious athletes looking for a real challenge.

Practical information

  • Foldable design: After your workout, simply fold the machine up. It then takes up minimal space against a wall or in a corner.
  • Easy to handle: Although it's stable, it's light enough for you to put away on your own after exercising.
  • Quiet operation: The mechanics are tuned so you can watch TV while you train without disturbing the neighbours.

Tip for beginners: To make your training as effective as possible, we'll gladly show you the correct technique when you pick it up. Once you find the right rhythm, rowing will become an addictive way to unwind and clear your head after work.
