This balance trainer, based on the well-known Bosu ball, is a surprisingly effective tool for improving body stability and balance and for strengthening your deep stabilising muscles. It was originally designed for rehabilitation centres, but it's just as useful at home or in the office, for example to help prevent back pain.

The trainer we rent out is about 2 centimetres smaller in radius than the original Bosu. We've tested it over the long term: the exercise options and results are exactly the same – only the price is lower.
