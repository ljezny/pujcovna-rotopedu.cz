Why rent an elliptical trainer:

Smooth motion

Unlike running, there's no hard impact of your feet on the ground.

Full-body workout

You engage your arms, legs, hips and core all at once.

High calorie burn

If your goal is weight loss, the elliptical is the ideal choice, since it works all your muscle groups.

Show more information

The elliptical trainer is also known as a cross trainer. According to specialists, it's one of the best machines for burning fat efficiently. Why? Because you work your whole body and drive the machine with your own body weight. Your energy expenditure is much higher than on an exercise bike, so weight loss is more effective.

Try before you buy: the perfect filter against “clothes racks”

Just like treadmills, elliptical trainers are prime candidates for becoming a home clothes rack. Renting from us is the smartest way to find out whether this type of exercise suits you. No risking tens of thousands on a machine – just try it for a few weeks and see if you get hooked.

Logistics and practical information

Our elliptical trainers are designed for home use, but they're still solid, stable machines:

  • Size and weight: Expect a weight of 30 to 50 kg and larger dimensions than a typical exercise bike. The extra weight is actually an advantage – it gives you the stability you need while exercising.
  • Will it fit in my car? Most of our elliptical trainers fit in a regular car (with the seats folded down), but it depends on the specific vehicle.
  • We're happy to advise: Not sure whether your car is big enough? Message or call us. We have years of experience with all kinds of cars and will tell you whether yours is suitable for a particular model.

Tip for successful training: the “only during my show” rule We've found that the best way to get used to the machine is to turn it into your private cinema. Pick a series you enjoy and promise yourself you'll only watch the next episode while working out. Your motivation to move gets linked to the joy of the next episode, and the time flies by.
